About Us
AL FALAK INDUSTRIAL EQUIP. TR. LLC., certified with the latest quality 
management system ISO 9001:2015, is a major supplier and stockiest for 
Pneumatic, Electrical, Mechanical,plumbing and Hydraulic equipments in 
switchgear applications, Industrial Automation Applications, water treatment 
plant, oil field marine and other industrial equipments for the UAE, Middle East, 
Central Asia and East Africa.
Our Expertise: 
Operating from our regional offices in UAE, We pride ourselves in 
providing with great support and services to the industries for a complete range 
of highly qualified equipment, parts, accessories and other engineering solutions.
With the attentiveness of our highly experienced staff, our innovative 
management and dedicated product knowledge in engineering and procurement 
sector put us to lead the industry, to meet customer demand and needs around 
the clock under 24/7 support and to emphasis our dedication and quality service.
Our Mission: 
To be the premier supplier of cutting-edge machinery and equipment, driving the
advancement of industries by providing solutions that enhance operational 
efficiency, sustainability, and long-term success.
Our Vision:
Our vision is to provide businesses with reliable and innovative machinery 
solutions that not only meet their immediate needs but also support their growth
and evolution. We are dedicated to delivering exceptional quality, unmatched 
customer service, and fostering lasting relationships built on trust and integrity.
